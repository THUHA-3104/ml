
# Bank Loan Project 
## Overview
The Bank Loan Analysis project aims to analyze loan application and repayment data to evaluate lending performance, identify risk factors, and provide business insights that support better lending decisions
# Business Problem
Banks process thousands of loan applications every month. Without proper analysis, it is difficult to understand:
- How much money is being lent?
- Which customers are most likely to default?
- Which loan purposes are riskier?
- Which states generate the highest loan volume?
- How has loan performance changed over time?

## Business Objectives
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders:
- Monitor loan performance
- Evaluate lending risk
- Analyze customer behavior
- Identify high-risk segments
- Support lending decisions

## Business Questions

This project aims to answer the following business questions:

### Lending Performance
- How many loan applications were received?
- What is the total amount of loans funded?
- How much payment has been received from borrowers?
- How have loan applications and funded amounts changed over time?

### Loan Portfolio
- Which states generate the highest loan volume?
- Which loan purposes account for the largest share of lending?
- What is the distribution of loans across different grades and terms?

### Customer Profile
- What are the characteristics of borrowers in terms of annual income, employment length, and home ownership?
- How do these characteristics relate to borrowing behavior?

### Credit Risk
- What percentage of loans are classified as Good Loans and Bad Loans?
- Which borrower segments have the highest default rates?
- Does loan grade accurately reflect credit risk?
- Are borrowers with higher DTI more likely to default?
- Do longer loan terms lead to higher default rates?

### Business Decision
- Which customer segments should the bank prioritize?
- Which loan categories require stricter approval policies?
- What actions could reduce the bank's credit risk while maintaining lending growth?
